# Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy: build notes

List: `drafts/kinnan.txt` (v1, 100 cards, 33 lands, 13 mana creatures + 7 rocks). Not on Moxfield yet. Card text read from Scryfall data of 2026-11-21 via card.py. Basis: EDHREC (22,906 decks) plus 188 Bracket 3 lists at $150-700 since 2025-06 (median 33-34 lands).

## Constraints
- Bracket 3. Game Changers: Seedborn Muse, Rhystic Study, Cyclonic Rift (max 3).
- Left out on purpose (all trip Bracket 4 or cEDH): Basalt Monolith / Grim Monolith (Kinnan + Monolith = infinite {C}), Freed from the Real, Pemmin's Aura (Kinnan + either = infinite), Bloom Tender + Freed, Hullbreaker Horror and Tidespout Tyrant (infinite with Sol Ring), Gaea's Cradle, fast mana (Moxen, Mana Vault), Fierce Guardianship, Force of Will, Consecrated Sphinx, Crop Rotation, Worldly Tutor.

## Plan
- Kinnan: whenever you tap a nonland permanent for mana, add one mana of any type that permanent produced. Each dork or rock nets +1. The ability ({5}{G}{U}) digs 5 for a non-Human creature, so the creature suite is all non-Human.
- T1 dork, T2 Kinnan or two dorks, T3-4 reach 8+ mana, T4-5 Craterhoof / Koma / Genesis Wave / Ulamog.
- Amplifiers: Seedborn Muse (untap on each opponent's turn, so tap again for instants, Chord, Kinnan activation), Nyxbloom Ancient (triples), Leyline of Abundance, Biomancer's Familiar (Kinnan's ability costs {3}{G}{U}), Rok-Tahk (every creature taps for {G}{G}).
- Wincons: Craterhoof, Koma, Ulamog, Ghalta, Worldspine Wurm, Kogla, Genesis Wave, clones of Craterhoof.
