## Gameplan

Jeskai prowess-voltron. **Shu Yun, the Silent Tempest** grows with every noncreature spell and gives double strike for {R/W}{R/W}; 25 one-drops and 24 two-drops (cantrips and pump spells) make each turn a chain of triggers. The engine is card flow that replaces itself: **Whirlwind of Thought**, **Archmage Emeritus**, **Jeskai Ascendancy**, **Combat Research** and **Staggering Insight** turn each cheap spell into another, and **Veyran, Voice of Duality** doubles every trigger (prowess, Ascendancy untaps, Whirlwind draws, **Guttersnipe**/**Fiery Inscription** burn, **Kykar**/**Monastery Mentor**/**Third Path Iconoclast** tokens).

Early: land, rock (7 rocks), a cheap prowess body. Turn 3-4: Shu Yun, then an engine or **Kediss, Emberclaw Familiar**. Mid: attack every turn, cantrip through the deck, hold up protection. Late: one swing with evasion + pump + a power doubler ends a player, or the whole table with Kediss.

30 lands + **Legion Leadership // Legion Stronghold**, 7 rocks, ~17 draw pieces, but only three real answers (**Swords to Plowshares**, **Counterspell**, **Boros Charm**) and no wipes. No Game Changers, no infinite combos, no tutors: Bracket 2 by contents, plays like a low 3 because it one-shots players from an empty-looking board. The list is 98 + commander, one slot open.

## Turn 1

Untapped land into **Monastery Swiftspear**, **Mother of Runes** or **Sol Ring**; otherwise **Ponder**/**Preordain** (sorceries, so use them now and save **Opt**/**Brainstorm** for instant speed). Ideal: T1 Swiftspear or Sol Ring, T2 Signet/Talisman or a two-drop body (**Sprite Dragon**, **Balmor, Battlemage Captain**, **Seeker of the Way**, Iconoclast, Kediss), T3 Shu Yun, T4 engine and start attacking. Sol Ring turn 1 means Shu Yun turn 2.

## Mulligan

Keep 3-4 lands (or 2 + a rock) plus either a draw engine (Whirlwind, Archmage, Ascendancy, Combat Research, Staggering Insight) or a 1-2 drop creature with two cantrips. Cantrips count as half a land. Dream keep: 3 lands, Swiftspear, Preordain, **Slip Through Space**, Whirlwind. Ship: all-pump hands (**Brute Force**, **Infuriate**, **Titan's Strength**, **Unleash Fury**) with no creature or draw, since pumps do nothing without Shu Yun and don't replace themselves; 2-landers with no rock; 5+ lands with no engine; slow hands built around **Medomai the Ageless** or **Elsha of the Infinite**.

## Wincons

1. **Commander damage one-shot** (most games). Attack with Shu Yun, make him unblockable (Slip Through Space, **Artful Dodge**, **Distortion Strike**, **Aqueous Form**, **Shadow Rift**, **Rogue's Passage**), pay for double strike on that trigger, then pump, then double last. Prowess resolves before each spell, so it's counted before a doubler. Example, 7 mana: Slip (4/3, double strike) → Legion Leadership (5, doubled to 10) → Unleash Fury (11 → 22). 22 + 22 = 44; 21 is lethal by rule 903.10a after the first hit. Cheaper: Slip + Titan's Strength + Unleash Fury = 18 ×2 = 36. **Invigorated Rampage**/**Monstrous Rage** trample substitutes for unblockable.
2. **Kediss table kill.** Same line with Kediss out: each combat-damage instance Shu Yun deals to one opponent is dealt to every other opponent. 44 to one player is 44 to all. Kediss damage is noncombat, so it isn't commander damage and doesn't trigger Combat Research; it just kills through life totals.
3. **Glamdring** free spells. With double strike, the first-strike hit lets you cast Unleash Fury or Boros Charm from hand free before regular damage. Glamdring and **Runechanter's Pike** make any prowess body a threat.
4. **Go-wide chip damage.** Mentor/Kykar/Iconoclast tokens with Ascendancy untaps and Balmor's team trample, plus Guttersnipe/Fiery Inscription (2 each per spell, 4 with Veyran) close games where Shu Yun keeps dying. **Sunforger** fetches Boros Charm, Legion Leadership, Unleash Fury, Swords, **Loran's Escape**, **Gods Willing**, **Emerge Unscathed** or **Blazing Crescendo** at instant speed.
5. **Medomai** extra turns: double strike from Shu Yun on an unblocked Medomai is two extra turns (he can't attack during them, Shu Yun can).

## Watch out for

Instant-speed removal in response to the pump chain is how you lose. Keep Gods Willing, Emerge Unscathed, Loran's Escape, untapped **Mother of Runes** or Boros Charm indestructible available before going in, and equip **Swiftfoot Boots** early. **Taigam, Ojutai Master** makes your instants and sorceries uncounterable, so counters only threaten Shu Yun himself. Deflecting Palm / Settle the Wreckage effects punish the all-in swing: against open white mana, kill with the minimum and keep a pump back. Wipes hurt the token side; Shu Yun plus cantrips rebuilds, so don't dump every engine at once.

Sequencing: evasion first, double strike on the first trigger, flat pumps, doublers last. Double strike granted after first-strike damage gives no extra hit unless the creature already had first strike (702.4d), so commit before blocks. Shu Yun's trigger targets any creature: Medomai, or a Mentor token wearing Glamdring. **Feather, the Redeemed** and Taigam's rebound both try to exile the resolving spell; you choose which applies (616.1): Feather returns it to hand, rebound recasts free next upkeep. Feather-loop Gods Willing/Loran's Escape every turn for permanent protection.

Commander removed repeatedly: at tax 5-7, pivot to Elsha (cantrips off the top at flash speed), **Narset, Enlightened Exile** (prowess for everyone, recasts pumps from any graveyard, including opponents'), or Balmor plus tokens. The table underestimates a 3/2 until the first 40-damage swing; after that expect focus, and with Kediss everyone is the target, so hold Boros Charm for indestructible rather than face damage.

## Notable

**Kediss** is a $5 partner card that turns a single kill into a table kill. **Veyran** is the best card after Shu Yun; protect it like a second commander. Runechanter's Pike and Glamdring count instants/sorceries in the graveyard, and Feather, rebound and flashback all keep them out, so those pumps are smaller than they look. **Scroll of the Masters** is the weakest slot and **Medomai** the slowest. **Blazing Crescendo** and **Monk Class** level 3 are good impulse draw with Elsha.

Open slot: **Shelter** (purchased, in the mail) is the obvious 99th card: protection that draws, and Feather loops it. Upgrades: **Path to Exile** (48.7% of lists, $1) for a second exile answer; **Deflecting Palm** (+21% synergy, $3), a Sunforger target that punishes other voltron decks; **Negate** to protect the swing. Cuts: Scroll of the Masters, **Boros Guildgate**/**Izzet Guildgate** (shocklands if budget allows), **Tranquil Cove**.
