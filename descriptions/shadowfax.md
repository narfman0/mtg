## Gameplan

**Shadowfax, Lord of Horses** is a budget Boros go-wide/commander-damage deck: Horses have haste, and every time Shadowfax attacks it drops a creature with lesser power from hand onto the battlefield tapped and attacking. The engine is Shadowfax plus counters. **Keleth, Sunmane Familiar** grows the commander by one +1/+1 counter per attack, which both raises the ceiling on what Shadowfax can cheat in (a 5/5 Shadowfax sneaks **Rearing Embermare**, a 6/6 sneaks **Guardian Sunmare**) and turns him into a real commander-damage clock once **Flaming Fist** or **Twinblade Blessing** gives him double strike. **Loyal Unicorn** makes attacking free (no combat damage to your creatures while you control your commander), **Team Avatar** rewards swinging with Shadowfax alone, and **Tori D'Avenant, Fury Rider** / **Wandering Musicians** / **Balefire Liege** turn the herd into a real alpha strike.

Early game is mana: 36 lands plus ~13 rocks/treasures/land-fetchers (**Sol Ring**, **Arcane Signet**, **Troop of Ponies**, **Thrór's Map**, **World Map**, **Dori, Bearer of Friends**, **Forging the Tyrite Sword**, **Glóin the Mighty**) so the commander lands on turn 4 with haste-relevant Horses around him. Mid game is Shadowfax attacking every turn, cheating a 3-power body each swing and stacking Keleth counters; **Guardian Sunmare** saddled fetches a 3-drop onto the battlefield each attack. Late game is a lethal double-strike Shadowfax or a **Team Avatar** / **Tori** alpha. The Dwarf package (**Thorin Oakenshield**, **Dáin, Lord of the Iron Hills**, **Óin the Brave**) wants an "enduring story" (three artifacts/legendaries/Sagas — trivial here) for ward 1, an attack tax, and a hasty looter.

Power level: bracket 2. No Game Changers, no tutors beyond Sunmare, no infinite combos, mostly sub-$1 cards. **Note: the list is 105 mainboard + commander (106); it needs six cuts — see Notable. Four of those are forced: Good-Fortune Unicorn, Lathiel, Prized Unicorn and Seraphic Steed are green and illegal in Shadowfax's R/W color identity.**

## Turn 1

Best: **Sol Ring**, then **World Map**, **Key to the Side-Door**, or **Steadfast Unicorn**. Ideal opener: land, Sol Ring; turn 2 **Arcane Signet** + **Keleth** (or **Troop of Ponies**, **Thrór's Map**), turn 3 Shadowfax with a 3-power creature in hand to cheat in on turn 4. Slower: turn 2 Keleth / **Diamond Mare** (name white), turn 3 rock, turn 4 Shadowfax swinging for a 5/5 and a free body.

## Mulligan

Keep 3-4 lands with one rock or fetcher (**Troop of Ponies**, **Thrór's Map**, **Changeling Wayfinder**, **Ambitious Farmhand**) plus a creature with power 3 or less to cheat in. Two lands is a keep only with Sol Ring or Signet in hand. Keleth, Loyal Unicorn or Flaming Fist make a hand a keep; Sunmare, Embermare, **Meteor Golem** and pump spells with no early plays do not. Ship 5-land hands with only tricks (**You're Not Alone**, **Take Up the Shield**, **Moment of Glory**) — the deck has plenty of mana already and needs bodies. Ship any hand with two of the green cards until they are cut.

## Wincons

1. **Commander damage** (most common). Shadowfax + Keleth counters + double strike from **Flaming Fist** (granted on attack) or **Twinblade Blessing** (flash it in after blocks). A 6/6 double-striker is 12; with **Team Avatar** (attack alone, +X/+X where X = creatures you control, counted on resolution — stack Shadowfax's trigger first so the cheated creature counts) a 5-creature board makes him 11/11 double strike = 22. **Pack a Punch** (+2 counters, trample) and **Key to the Side-Door** (unblockable) close it. Two connections kill.
2. **Go-wide alpha**. **Tori** (+1/+1 to attackers, untaps white attackers), **Wandering Musicians**, **Balefire Liege** anthem, **Lorehold Charm** trample mode, **Steadfast Unicorn** pump. **Loyal Unicorn** makes every swing risk-free, so keep attacking with everything every turn.
3. **Toughness damage**. **Bill the Pony**'s Food lets **Guardian Sunmare** (5/5), **Rearing Embermare** (4/5) or Musicians (2/5) hit for toughness; small but free.
4. **Balefire Liege** pings: 3 damage per red spell cast is a real reach on a 4-turn clock. No infinite combos exist in the list.

## Watch out for

- Creatures cheated in by Shadowfax never "attacked" (rule 508.4), so **Ash**, **Nori**, **Tori**, **Musicians**, **Gilded Ghoda** and **Guardian Sunmare** do not trigger the turn they come in — cast the attack-trigger creatures normally and cheat in bodies like **Meteor Golem** (free removal), **Solemn Simulacrum** or **Balefire Liege** instead.
- Shadowfax dies to everything: 4 toughness, no protection except ward 1 from **Thorin** and **Valorous Stance** / **Take Up the Shield** / **Airbender's Reversal** (exile-and-recast for {2}). Hold W up when possible. After two commander deaths, tax is 9 — lean on **Tori** and the anthems and play a fair Boros midrange game.
- Board wipes hurt; **Destined Confrontation** and **Fall of the First Civilization** III are your own selective wipes — pre-plan which 3 permanents / 4 power you keep (Shadowfax alone is 4 power, so counters break Confrontation).
- **Loyal Unicorn** only prevents combat damage to your creatures; it does not help against removal or blockers eating damage. **Dáin**'s tax discourages crack-backs; **Thorin**'s ward 1 stops cheap spot removal.
- **Kirol, History Buff** prepares whenever cards leave your graveyard: flashback (**Daydream**, **Moment of Glory**, **Laughing Mad**, **Pursue the Past**), **Helping Hand**, **Lorehold Charm** reanimation, **Barkform Harvester**'s ability.
- Politically the deck looks harmless; the double-strike turn is the reveal, so don't show Twinblade Blessing early.

## Notable

**Six cuts to reach 99**: (1-4) **Good-Fortune Unicorn**, **Lathiel, the Bounteous Dawn**, **Prized Unicorn**, **Seraphic Steed** — green, not legal here at all; (5) **Temple of the False God** — with 36 lands and a dozen rocks the deck is mana-flooded, and Temple is dead early; (6) **Boros Cluestone** — the weakest rock (5.6% inclusion), redundant with Signet, Waterskin, Blitzball, Trove and Opaline Unicorn.

Unusual but good: **Meteor Golem** at 3 power is a free Vindicate off Shadowfax. **Involuntary Employment** steals a blocker and makes Treasure. **Blitzball** draws two whenever a legend connects, which is every turn here.

Underperforming: **Ronom Unicorn**, **Brightmare** and **Opaline Unicorn** are filler — only Horses get haste from Shadowfax, Unicorns do not.

Upgrades (all under $1 on EDHREC): **Calamity, Galloping Inferno** (67%), **Shield Mare**, **Lightning Mare**, **Thundermare**, **Armored Warhorse**, **Brightfield Mustang** are on-plan Horses that replace the green cards; **Swords to Plowshares** / **Path to Exile** for removal; **Command Tower** and **Boros Signet** over Guildgate / Cluestone.
