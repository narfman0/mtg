## Gameplan

Five-color Sliver tribal with **Sliver Overlord** as a repeatable tutor: every {3} finds the lord the board needs. Mana lords (**Gemhide Sliver**, **Manaweft Sliver**, **Cryptolith Rite**) turn every 2-drop into a dork; anthems (**Muscle**, **Sinew**, **Predatory**, **Bonesplitter**, **Megantic Sliver**, and above all **Sliver Legion**) make the swarm lethal; keyword lords (**Bonescythe Sliver** double strike, **Galerider**/**Cloudshredder Sliver** flying, **Shifting Sliver**, **Two-Headed Sliver**, **Horned Sliver**) make it unblockable; protection lords (**Crystalline Sliver**, **Sliver Hivelord**, **Diffusion Sliver**, **Hibernation Sliver**, **Pulmonic Sliver**, **Root Sliver**) keep it alive.

**Training Grounds** turns the pile into a machine: Overlord tutors for {1}, **Sliver Queen** makes a token for {1}, **Necrotic Sliver** destroys a permanent for {1}. Cards come from **Kindred Discovery** (name Sliver), **Synapse Sliver**, **Phyrexian Arena**, **Sylvan Library**, **Herald's Horn** and **Homing Sliver** slivercycling.

Typical game: turns 1-2 fix mana and land a mana Sliver, turn 3-4 Overlord, then tutor the lord that solves the current problem while adding two Slivers a turn until Legion or the Queen loop ends it around turn 7. 35 lands, 7 ramp, 5 draw engines, 9 removal pieces, 4 counters, and **Crippling Fear** (name Sliver) as a one-sided wipe. Curve is low: 24 two-drops, average nonland MV 2.9.

Bracket 4: five Game Changers (**Cyclonic Rift**, **Demonic Tutor**, **Vampiric Tutor**, **Fierce Guardianship**, **Teferi's Protection**), fetch/shock mana base, tutorable infinite combo.

## Turn 1

Fetch into an untapped shock plus **Galerider Sliver** or **Striking Sliver**; **Sol Ring**; **Training Grounds** if Overlord is coming turn 3-4; **Sylvan Library**. **Cavern of Souls**, **Unclaimed Territory**, **Secluded Courtyard** and **Sliver Hive** are ideal first lands since nearly every spell is a Sliver.

Ideal opener: land, Sol Ring, Training Grounds. Turn 2: land, Gemhide or Manaweft plus a one-drop Sliver. Turn 3: Overlord, tutor for {1} at end of turn. Alternative: end-of-turn-1 Vampiric Tutor for the missing piece, Gemhide turn 2, Overlord turn 3 with Gemhide tapping.

## Mulligan

Keep 3-4 lands, at least two making more than one color (fetches count). Two-landers only with Sol Ring, **Arcane Signet** or **Fellwar Stone** plus a 2-mana Sliver. A keep needs a mana Sliver, a rock, or Training Grounds, plus at least one cheap Sliver. Five lands with Overlord and a tutor is fine; the commander is the engine.

Ship: three or more five-drops (Legion, Queen, Hivelord, **Patriarch's Bidding**, Kindred Discovery, Pulmonic) with no acceleration; all tapped lands (Triomes, **Path of Ancestry**) and no one-drop; all interaction (**Counterspell**, **Mana Drain**, **Swords to Plowshares**, **Path to Exile**) and no creature. This deck wins by being ahead on board, not by trading one-for-one.

## Wincons

1. **Lord beatdown** (most games). Legion gives each Sliver +1/+1 per other Sliver; with six out every body is at least 6/6, and **Shared Animosity** adds +5/+0 on the swing. With Bonescythe and an evasion lord, three attackers kill a player. Menace plus Shifting means only Slivers can block. **Syphon Sliver** lifelink keeps you out of reach.

2. **Queen loop.** Sliver Queen, Training Grounds, a mana lord (Gemhide, Manaweft or Cryptolith Rite), and a haste source (**Heart Sliver**, **Blur Sliver**, Cloudshredder, **Thrumming Hivepool**). Queen's ability costs {1}; the token enters with haste and taps for one mana (required: rule 302.6 stops a summoning-sick creature using a {T} ability), which pays for the next token. Net zero mana, arbitrarily many tokens. Overlord tutors Queen, the mana lord and the haste lord for {1} each once Training Grounds is out; Demonic/Vampiric find Training Grounds. With **Lavabelly Sliver** every token ETB pings, so infinite tokens is infinite damage. Without it, stop with a few untapped tokens: with Legion out they are arbitrarily large, and next turn the whole army untaps.

3. **Necrotic Vindicate engine.** Same setup with Necrotic Sliver: {1} makes a token, it taps to pay for the next, {1} sacrifices the first to destroy any permanent. One external mana per permanent destroyed.

4. **Triumph of the Hordes.** With four attackers and Bonescythe, unblocked infect is doubled: a 3-power double-striker with the +1/+1 gives 8 poison. Shifting and Two-Headed make blocks impossible. Usually one to two players from nowhere.

5. **Rebuild**: Patriarch's Bidding naming Sliver returns the hive after a wrath; Pulmonic puts dead Slivers on top of the library and Herald's Horn draws them.

## Watch out for

Wipes are the real threat; the deck overextends by design. Answers in order: Hivelord (beats destroy, not exile or -X/-X), Teferi's Protection, Hibernation (pay 2 life per Sliver in response), Bidding to rebuild. Counter Farewell and Toxic Deluge with Counterspell/Mana Drain/**Swan Song**; Fierce Guardianship is free once Overlord is out.

Targeted removal on Overlord is annoying, not fatal: Diffusion taxes it, Crystalline gives shroud. But Crystalline is symmetric: it stops Overlord's steal from targeting anything and stops your own Swords/Path/**Constricting Sliver** exile from hitting Slivers. Hibernation still works because it does not target. If Overlord keeps dying, Homing Sliver slivercycling is the backup tutor.

**Harmonic Sliver's trigger is mandatory** and every Sliver entering after it gets the ETB. Once opponents have no artifacts or enchantments, each new Sliver, including every Queen token, must destroy one of yours (Training Grounds, Cryptolith Rite, Kindred Discovery, Sol Ring). Rule 603.3d only spares you when there is no legal target at all. Bounce Harmonic with Hibernation or sacrifice it to Necrotic before comboing.

Kindred Discovery's draw is not optional: with the Queen loop you deck yourself. Stop before the library empties, or make sure Lavabelly ends the game first.

**Sentinel Sliver** vigilance lets the team attack and still tap for mana; **Venom Sliver** plus first strike means nothing blocks profitably; **Quick Sliver** is symmetric but only matters in a mirror.

You become the archenemy the moment Legion or Queen resolves. Sandbag one of Hivelord/Teferi's, and lead with Crippling Fear to clear opposing boards before the alpha strike.

## Notable

Patriarch's Bidding and Crippling Fear are off-meta (26% and 8% of lists) but exactly right here; Shared Animosity is not on the Overlord page at all and is a genuine finisher.

Underperforming: Constricting Sliver (six mana), **Brood Sliver** (five mana for tokens the deck already makes), **Leeching Sliver** (1 life per attack next to these anthems), and **Chromatic Lantern** (slow with 35 fixing lands).

Upgrades: **The First Sliver** (cascade on every Sliver, 75% of lists, ~$40) is the biggest gap. **Intruder Alarm** turns Queen plus two mana Slivers into infinite mana and tokens with no haste needed. **Rhystic Study** is the draw engine the deck lacks. Cheap swaps: **Crypt Sliver** or **Winged Sliver** for Constricting, **Heroic Intervention** for Leeching.
