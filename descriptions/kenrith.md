## Gameplan

Despite the title this is not a lands deck: it is a cEDH five-color Kenrith combo list built on fast mana, mana dorks and a cheap interaction suite. The plan is to land a dork engine early (**Bloom Tender**, **Birds of Paradise**, **Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy** doubling them), draw off tax pieces (**Rhystic Study**, **Mystic Remora**, **Esper Sentinel**, **Smothering Tithe**, **Pollywog Prodigy**), then tutor into one of two engines: an **Underworld Breach** storm turn, or the creature loop **Village Bell-Ringer** + **Warren Soultrader** with **Kenrith, the Returned King** reanimating the Bell-Ringer every time it dies. Kenrith himself is mostly a mana sink and a finisher, not the engine.

Early game (turns 1-2): fast mana (Petal, Moxen, **Sol Ring**, **Mana Vault**, **Ancient Tomb**/**City of Traitors**) into a dork plus a draw engine, holding a free counter. Mid game (turns 3-4): resolve Kinnan or **Jeskai Ascendancy**/**Valley Floodcaller**, tutor with **Demonic Tutor**/**Vampiric Tutor**/**Imperial Seal**/**Gamble**/**Enlightened Tutor**, and protect the turn with **Silence**/**Orim's Chant**/**Ranger-Captain of Eos**. The deck has 27 lands, 17 mana producers, 15 counterspells, 8 tutors and zero wipes; the curve is 26 one-drops and 20 two-drops with only 6 cards above 3.

Power level: bracket 5 / cEDH. Eighteen Game Changers: Ancient Tomb, Chrome Mox, Crop Rotation, Demonic Tutor, Enlightened Tutor, Fierce Guardianship, Force of Will, Gamble, Imperial Seal, Lion's Eye Diamond, Mana Vault, Mox Diamond, Opposition Agent, Orcish Bowmasters, Rhystic Study, Smothering Tithe, Underworld Breach, Vampiric Tutor.

## Turn 1

Best openers: land + **Lotus Petal**/**Chrome Mox**/**Mox Diamond** into Bloom Tender, Birds or Kinnan; Ancient Tomb + Sol Ring or Mana Vault; **Gemstone Caverns** on the draw; **Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer** or Esper Sentinel into an open board; Mystic Remora when you are not first. An ideal first two turns: T1 dual + Petal + Bloom Tender, T2 land + Kinnan + Rhystic Study with **Force of Will** or **Fierce Guardianship** in hand, so turn 3 has 6+ mana and a tutor.

## Mulligan

Keep 2-3 lands (1 land is fine with two fast-mana pieces), at least one mana producer beyond lands, and one of: a tutor, a draw engine, or a free counter. Snap keeps: any hand with Kinnan + a dork, Breach + a tutor, or Rhystic Study + Petal. Ship: four-plus lands with no action; all interaction and no mana; Kenrith plus nothing that makes mana; **Lion's Eye Diamond** without Breach or a tutor; a hand of combo pieces with no way to reach five mana. Prefer a 6 with fast mana over a clunky 7.

## Wincons

1. **Underworld Breach + Brain Freeze + Lion's Eye Diamond.** With Breach out, escape LED from the graveyard ({0} + exile three cards), sacrifice it for UUU, escape **Brain Freeze** ({1}{U} + exile three) targeting yourself; each Brain Freeze storm copy mills three, so the graveyard grows faster than escape eats it. Net +1 mana per cycle (**Dark Ritual** escapes for +2). Mill your library, then Brain Freeze each opponent's library and finish by casting Kenrith and using {3}{U} on each opponent: drawing from an empty library loses on the next state-based check (rule 704.5b). Jeskai Ascendancy or Valley Floodcaller plus Bloom Tender/Kinnan makes every escaped Petal or Mox untap the dorks, so **Lotus Petal**/**Chrome Mox** also loop; Floodcaller lets all of this happen at instant speed and untaps **Mockingbird** copies (they are Birds). **Shifting Woodland** (delirium) or **Noxious Revival** gets Breach back.
2. **Kenrith + Village Bell-Ringer + Warren Soultrader + dorks making 5 mana.** {4}{B} reanimates Bell-Ringer, it untaps all creatures, Soultrader sacrifices it for a Treasure, repeat. Bloom Tender with Kinnan and four colors on board makes 8; **Cryptolith Rite**/**Enduring Vitality** with five creatures also works. **Biomancer's Familiar** drops the loop to {2}{B}; the 1 life per sacrifice is covered by Kenrith's {2}{W} for 5. Infinite mana and Treasures, then kill with Kenrith {4}{B} on **Orcish Bowmasters** plus Soultrader sacrifice (1 damage per loop) or draw the deck with {3}{U}. **Phantasmal Image**/Mockingbird copying Bell-Ringer give a second untap body.
3. **Agatha's Soul Cauldron shortcut.** If Kenrith dies (Soultrader can sacrifice him), you may leave him in the graveyard (903.9a is optional); Cauldron exiles him and its trigger puts a counter on Bloom Tender, which now has every Kenrith ability payable with any mana, and Familiar makes them cheap. Same loop, no commander tax.
4. Fair plan: Ragavan, Esper Sentinel and Bowmasters chip in while the tax enchantments bury the table; Kenrith's {3}{U} refills in the late game.

## Watch out for

Graveyard hate (Rest in Peace, Dauthi Voidwalker, Bojuka Bog) kills lines 1 and 3; Drannith Magistrate stops escape casts; Cursed Totem/Collector Ouphe turn off dorks and Kenrith. Opposing Bowmasters punish Rhystic draws and the Kenrith draw loop. Hold **Deflecting Swat** and **Talon Gates of Madara** ({4} from hand phases a creature out) for Kinnan and Bloom Tender, your real engines. Breach sacrifices itself at the end step: do not cast it without the pieces. **Pact of Negation** kills you next upkeep. **Mindbreak Trap**/**Flusterstorm** are your answers to someone else's storm turn; **Opposition Agent** plus **Scheming Symmetry** steals a tutor target, and **Wan Shi Tong, Librarian** draws off every fetch. If Kenrith keeps eating removal, pivot to line 1 or 3, which need him only as the final draw. Politically, Kenrith's draw and lifegain buy goodwill; **Tataru Taru** and **Lotho, Corrupt Shirriff** look harmless.

## Notable

Odd but functional: **Badgermole Cub** (extra G on every dork tap, doubled by Kinnan), **The Cabbage Merchant** (Foods into mana), **Amphibian Downpour** (storm removal), **Steal Enchantment** (takes an opposing Rhystic Study), **Crop Rotation** as a toolbox for Talon Gates, **Emergence Zone**, **Mistrise Village** and **Boseiju, Who Endures**. Underperformers: **Commandeer** and **Misdirection** (five- and seven-mana cards in a deck of one-drops), **Sevinne's Reclamation**, **Firestorm**. Upgrades: **Thassa's Oracle** is in the average list and turns the Breach self-mill into a clean kill without needing Kenrith on board; **Training Grounds** (37% synergy) stacks with Familiar to make every Kenrith ability one or two mana. Cut Commandeer and Badgermole Cub first.
