## Gameplan

Golgari reanimator-combo around **Meren of Clan Nel Toth**: a low curve (avg nonland MV 2.27, 30 lands + 8 mana creatures), 14 tutors, and a pile of free sacrifice outlets (**Viscera Seer**, **Carrion Feeder**, **Ashnod's Altar**, **Phyrexian Altar**, **Warren Soultrader**, **Yawgmoth, Thran Physician**, **Phyrexian Tower**, **High Market**). Every death feeds Meren experience and her end-step trigger rebuys what you sacrificed. Value engines: **Skullclamp**, **Grim Haruspex**, **Morbid Opportunist**, **Braids, Arisen Nightmare**, Yawgmoth. Drain layer: **Blood Artist**, **Zulaport Cutthroat**, **Marionette Apprentice**, **Disciple of the Vault**.

Early: dork or fast mana, then either cheat a fatty (**Entomb**/**Buried Alive** into **Reanimate**/**Animate Dead**/**Necromancy**) or land Meren turn 3 with an outlet. Mid: Meren loops **Sakura-Tribe Elder**, **Eternal Witness**, **Spore Frog** and **Accursed Marauder** while tutors assemble a combo. Late: **Protean Hulk** dies, or **Mikaeus, the Unhallowed** / **Melira, Sylvok Outcast** meets **Lesser Masticore** or **Walking Ballista**, and the table dies to drain triggers.

Six Game Changers (**Ancient Tomb**, **Demonic Tutor**, **Imperial Seal**, **Vampiric Tutor**, **Natural Order**, **Survival of the Fittest**) plus turn-1 reanimation and Hulk lines make this Bracket 4. No counterspells and little free interaction, so high-power rather than true cEDH.

## Turn 1

- Best: Swamp + **Dark Ritual** (or **Lotus Petal**) → Entomb + Reanimate on **Archon of Cruelty** (8 life; Archon refunds 3 and draws every swing). Ritual into Buried Alive (Hulk/Archon/Mikaeus) sets up a turn-2 reanimation.
- Normal: a dork (**Llanowar Elves**, **Elvish Mystic**, **Fyndhorn Elves**, **Birds of Paradise**, **Elves of Deep Shadow**, **Deathrite Shaman**), **Sol Ring**, or Skullclamp (turn 2: clamp a dork, draw two).
- Ideal opener: T1 dork, T2 Sakura-Tribe Elder or Viscera Seer plus a two-drop, T3 Meren with an outlet already online and the first experience counter that end step.

## Mulligan

- Keep 2-4 lands plus one of: a dork/Sol Ring, a tutor (Demonic, Vampiric, **Diabolic Intent**, Entomb), or an engine (Skullclamp, Seer, Braids). One-landers with Petal/Ritual and Entomb + Reanimate are keeps.
- Auto-keep: Entomb or Buried Alive with a reanimation spell; dork + Skullclamp; two lands + dork + Meren.
- Count black sources: Mikaeus and **Agadeem's Awakening** need BBB; **Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth** fixes everything.
- Ship: 5+ lands and no action; Hulk/Archon/Mikaeus stuck in hand with no discard outlet (Survival, Lesser Masticore, **Bone Shards**, Yawgmoth) and no tutor; all removal, no development. Meren alone doesn't make a keep — she's a 4-mana do-nothing until something dies.

## Wincons

1. **Persist/undying drain loop (most common).** Lesser Masticore + a free outlet + Melira (persist returns it with no -1/-1 counter) *or* Mikaeus (undying's +1/+1 and persist's -1/-1 cancel per CR 704.5q, so it alternates forever). Every death triggers Blood Artist / Zulaport / Marionette Apprentice / Disciple of the Vault (Masticore is an artifact). Ashnod's or Phyrexian Altar makes it infinite mana; Haruspex or Yawgmoth draws the deck; Soultrader makes infinite Treasures at 1 life each (Blood Artist refunds it).
2. **Protean Hulk.** Natural Order (sac a dork), **Fiend Artisan** X=7, **Green Sun's Zenith** X=7, or Reanimate/Animate Dead/Necromancy/**Apprentice Necromancer**/**Footsteps of the Goryo** on an entombed Hulk, then kill it with any outlet. Pile (exactly 6 MV): Viscera Seer + Melira + Lesser Masticore + Disciple of the Vault — sac Masticore forever, Disciple drains everyone. With Mikaeus already out: Walking Ballista (X=0) + Seer + Blood Artist + Zulaport + Carrion Feeder. With Yawgmoth out: Melira + Masticore + Blood Artist. At 7 experience Meren recurs Hulk every end step. Backup: **Shifting Woodland** with delirium copies Hulk from your graveyard; sacrifice the land-Hulk and the dies trigger fires.
3. **Mikaeus + Walking Ballista + outlet.** Remove the counter to ping, sacrifice, undying returns it with a counter, repeat: infinite damage. Mikaeus + Yawgmoth + two other non-Humans is infinite draw and death triggers (Yawgmoth's -1/-1 counter cancels the undying counter).
4. **Yawgmoth + Melira + Masticore**: 1 life per loop draws the deck and shrinks an opposing creature; add any drain body to close.
5. **Fair plan.** Early Archon under **Lightning Greaves**, Braids taxing the table every end step, Skullclamp on persist/undying bodies, Meren grinding Elder and Witness. Slow, but wins attrition against removal-heavy tables.

## Watch out for

- **Graveyard exile** (Rest in Peace, Bojuka Bog, Farewell, opposing Dauthi) is the real enemy. Hold Necromancy (flash) to answer a Bog; **Endurance** can target *yourself* in response to targeted exile to tuck the yard instead of losing it. **Force of Vigor**, **Haywire Mite**, **Abrupt Decay** and **Boseiju** answer RIP and Grafdigger's Cage.
- Cursed Totem / Linvala turn off Seer, Feeder, Ballista, Soultrader, Yawgmoth and the dorks; the Altars, Tower and High Market still work.
- Sequencing: Braids' sacrifice and Meren's return both trigger at the beginning of the end step, and Meren chooses her target as the trigger goes on the stack (CR 603.3), so a creature sacrificed to Braids won't return the same turn. Sac to Seer in main phase 2 first, then let Braids eat something else.
- Yawgmoth's proliferate hits *players*: {B}{B}, discard Hulk or Archon = +1 experience and a reanimation target in the yard.
- Protect Meren/Yawgmoth with Greaves or **Sylvan Safekeeper**. If Meren keeps dying, don't recast into open mana; the deck combos without her via Buried Alive + **Victimize** or the Mikaeus/Melira lines.
- Life is a resource (Reanimate, Ancient Tomb, **Snuff Out**, **Toxic Deluge**, Yawgmoth, Vampiric). Track it against Archon/Blood Artist refunds and stay above ~10 into open boards.
- Wipes usually help you: Deluge with an outlet in response cashes your board into triggers and experience.
- Politics: turn-1 Archon paints a target; a turn-3 Meren looping Spore Frog looks harmless. Lead with value, not Hulk, unless you go off the same turn.

## Notable

- **Dauthi Voidwalker** is graveyard hate plus a free spell when sacrificed; **Culling the Weak** turns a chump body into a Reanimate + Mikaeus turn; **Chthonian Nightmare** with 3 energy recurs a 3-drop and bounces to replay.
- Softest slots: **Growing Rites of Itlimoc** (wants four creatures on a board that sacrifices everything) and **Phyrexian Arena** (slow for this speed).
- Upgrades (EDHREC): **Plaguecrafter** / **Fleshbag Marauder** (under $1) are repeatable edicts Meren loops every turn; **Gray Merchant of Asphodel** ($4) is a Hulk-independent finisher; **Pitiless Plunderer** ($5) makes every loop mana-positive. From the maybeboard, **Contamination** with Meren returning fodder each turn is a real lock but draws hate; **Gaea's Cradle** ($1,600) only as a proxy. Cuts: Growing Rites, Phyrexian Arena, or Snuff Out if the table is mostly black creatures.
