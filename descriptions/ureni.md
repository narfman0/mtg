## Gameplan

Temur Dragons: ramp hard, land a cost-reduced **Ureni of the Unwritten** around turn 4, then turn every Dragon cast into two bodies and a burst of damage. Ureni's enter-or-attack trigger digs 8 deep for a free Dragon; with 27 Dragons in the 99 that hits about 90% of the time and fires every combat. There is no infinite combo. The deck wins by weight of Dragons and the damage they deal while entering.

Three layers stack. Cost: **Dragonspeaker Shaman**, **Urza's Incubator**, **Dragonlord's Servant**, **Nogi, Draco-Zealot**, **Sarkhan, Soul Aflame**, **Temur Battlecrier**, later **Dracogenesis**. Multipliers: **Miirym, Sentinel Wyrm** copies every nontoken Dragon, **Lathliss, Dragon Queen** adds a 5/5 per Dragon, **Roaming Throne** (name Dragon) doubles the triggers of Ureni, Miirym, Lathliss, **Scourge of Valkas** and **Terror of the Peaks**. Payoffs: **Dragon Tempest**, Valkas and Terror turn each entry into damage, and **Twinflame Tyrant** doubles all of it.

Typical game: turns 1-3 are rocks, a land-search sorcery and a reducer. Turn 4-5 is Ureni or a five-drop Dragon; then one or two Dragons a turn plus the free one off Ureni's attack, with cards from **Elemental Bond**, **Garruk's Uprising** and **Temur Ascendancy**. By turn 7-8 you're swinging 30+ power in the air or pinging the table out. 36 lands, 13 ramp pieces, 6 reducers, average nonland mana value 4.2. No Game Changers; strong Bracket 3 (fast mana is only **Sol Ring** and **Mox Jasper**, but **Mana Drain**, **Deflecting Swat** and the fetch/shock manabase sit above casual).

## Turn 1

**Birds of Paradise** or Sol Ring off an untapped land; crack **Misty Rainforest** / **Scalding Tarn** / **Wooded Foothills** for a shock to fix colours. Mox Jasper is dead until a Dragon is out. Ideal opener: T1 Sol Ring, T2 Dragonspeaker Shaman (needs RR) or **Nature's Lore** / **Three Visits** (untapped **Breeding Pool** / **Stomping Ground** / **Cinder Glade**) or **Farseek** (**Steam Vents**, **Ketria Triome**), T3 Urza's Incubator or **Cultivate**, T4 Ureni for {G}{U}{R} with Shaman + Incubator, or Terror / **Goldspan Dragon** into Ureni turn 5. A turn-2 **Deceptive Frostkite** switches on Mox Jasper and **Temple of the Dragon Queen**.

## Mulligan

Keep 3-4 lands, or 2 lands plus Sol Ring / Birds plus a ramp spell. A keep needs green by turn 2 (all the land search is green) and one of: a reducer, a two-mana ramp spell, or a draw engine (Bond, Uprising, Ascendancy). Auto-keep: Sol Ring or Birds plus a reducer and lands; three lands, a green ramp spell and a five-drop Dragon. Ship: three lands and three 6+ drops with no ramp; five or six lands and only Dragons; hands whose only acceleration is Mox Jasper or **Carnelian Orb of Dragonkind** with no Dragon; hands whose coloured sources are all restricted lands (**Haven of the Spirit Dragon**, **Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon**, **Cavern of Souls**).

## Wincons

1. **Dragon combat.** The usual route. Ureni's attack trigger, Lathliss and **Utvara Hellkite** tokens, Miirym copies, haste from Ascendancy, **Frostcliff Siege** (Temur), **Rhythm of the Wild**, Tempest or **Lightning Greaves**. **Atarka, World Render** gives every attacking Dragon double strike; **Scourge of the Throne** untaps the team and adds a combat when it first attacks the life leader (Ureni attacks again, another free Dragon). **Balefire Dragon** clears one player's blockers, **Old Gnawbone** and **Ancient Copper Dragon** turn hits into Treasure, **Broodcaller Scourge** cheats in a permanent per damaged player.
2. **Commander damage.** Ureni is a 7/7 flying trampler. Twinflame makes it 14; add Atarka's double strike for 28, lethal in one swing (903.10a). Lathliss's {1}{R} pump and Frostcliff's +1/+0 cover a missing piece.
3. **Entry burn.** Tempest and Valkas each deal X (Dragons you control) per Dragon entering; Terror deals the entrant's power. Miirym doubles the entries, Throne doubles the creature triggers (not Tempest, an enchantment), Twinflame doubles the damage. With Miirym, Tempest and Valkas out and six Dragons on board, one Dragon cast is two entries and about 30 damage to any targets, 60 with Twinflame. **Dragonhawk, Fate's Tempest** adds 2 per unplayed exiled card to each opponent.
4. **Dracogenesis** casts every Dragon in hand and every **Thundermane Dragon** top-deck for free, and reducers still eat the commander tax (601.2f: reductions apply to additional costs too), so recasting Ureni stays cheap.

## Watch out for

Wipes hurt most: hold **Heroic Intervention** once three Dragons are down, and nothing here survives your own **Blasphemous Act**. Spot removal on Ureni: Deflecting Swat is free with any commander out; Greaves gives shroud but then your own **Will of the Temur** can't copy Ureni. Counterspells: cast Ureni with Cavern (name Dragon) or **Mistrise Village** mana, or after Rhythm. ETB hate (Torpor Orb, Hushbringer) blanks Ureni, Tempest, Terror and Miirym; answer it first with **Beast Within** / **Chaos Warp** / **Hammerhead Tyrant** bounce.

Sequencing: get Tempest, Valkas, Terror, Miirym and Throne out before Ureni so its free Dragon triggers them. Frostkite copying a legendary Dragon hits the legend rule (704.5j); copy Terror, Twinflame or Valkas. Sarkhan may copy Ureni until end of turn as a legendary "Sarkhan" 7/7 with the attack trigger; with haste that's a second dig. **Klauth, Unrivaled Ancient** and **Savage Ventmaw** mana lasts the turn: cast Dragons post-combat, from the top with Thundermane. **Hellkite Courser** drops Ureni in from the command zone with haste, no tax paid or added, and returns it at end step. **Encroaching Dragonstorm** bounces itself when a Dragon enters; recast it for two more lands. **Ureni, the Song Unending** has protection from white and black: Swords and Path can't target it, W/B creatures can't block it.

When Ureni dies repeatedly, stop recasting: 27 Dragons plus reducers run fine without it, Courser cheats it back, Dracogenesis casts it for the reduced tax alone. Ureni draws hate on sight; keep Swat mana visible, and remind the table Balefire only punishes the player it hits.

## Notable

**Roaming Throne**, **Ancient Silver Dragon**, Klauth and **Marang River Regent** are 25-35% plays on EDHREC but all earn their slot (Marang's double bounce and its Omen draw are both live). Mana Drain and Deflecting Swat are the least-played cards for this commander and the best interaction here. Underperformers: **Parapet Thrasher** is a 4/3 that needs Dragons to connect first; **Rapid Hybridization** hands out a 3/3 and rarely beats a wrath; Frostcliff Siege competes with Ascendancy and Rhythm for the haste slot. Upgrades: **Mosswort Bridge** (61% of lists, hideaway a Dragon on a land slot), **Thunderbreak Regent** (48%, punishes targeting your Dragons), **Selvala's Stampede** (47%), or **Reality Shift** over Hybridization. **Frontier Siege** is the other high-inclusion pick: two extra mana each main phase or Dragon fights.
