## Gameplan

Naya Cats-and-Dogs go-wide. **Rin and Seri, Inseparable** turns every Cat spell into a free Dog token and every Dog spell into a Cat token, and the deck stacks tribal lords on top of that so 1/1s hit as 3/3s and 4/4s: **King of the Pride** (+2/+1 Cats), **Feline Sovereign**, **Regal Caracal** (+1/+1 and lifelink), **Kaheera, the Orphanguard**, **Pack Leader** (Dogs), plus global anthems **Dictate of Heliod**, **Collective Blessing** and **True Conviction**. **Jetmir, Nexus of Revels** is the finisher-lord: at nine creatures the whole team gets +3/+0, vigilance, trample and double strike.

The engine hub is **Maskwood Nexus**: with it out, every creature you control (and every creature spell you cast) is both a Cat and a Dog, so each creature cast triggers both halves of Rin and Seri (two tokens per spell), every lord pumps everything, and Rin and Seri's tap ability deals damage equal to your *entire* creature count. **Jinnie Fay, Jetmir's Second** upgrades all tokens into 2/2 haste Cats or 3/1 vigilance Dogs. **Kutzil, Malamet Exemplar** (opponents can't cast spells on your turn) and **Marisi, Breaker of the Coil** (no spells during combat, goad on hit) make your alpha strikes uninterruptible.

Typical game: turns 1-3 ramp and two-drop Dogs; turn 3-4 Rin and Seri plus a lord; turns 5-7 grow to 8-12 bodies while holding a protection spell, then close with an overrun or Jetmir's double strike. 36 lands + 9 rocks with a curve around 3 means it rarely stumbles on mana, but interaction is thin: one creature removal spell (**Swords to Plowshares**) and no wipes.

Power: no Game Changers, no tutors, no two-card infinite. Bracket 2 (a strong upgraded precon), edging toward 3 with Kutzil/Marisi.

## Turn 1

Best: **Sol Ring** (Rin and Seri turn 3), **Ajani's Welcome**, **Selfless Savior**, or a scry Temple / lifegain tapland. Ideal opener: land, Sol Ring; turn 2 land + Signet/Talisman + **Spirited Companion** or **Loyal Warhound** (which fetches a Plains if you're behind on lands); turn 3 Rin and Seri or **Kutzil**/**King of the Pride**, turn 4 the other.

## Mulligan

- Keep 3-4 lands (2 with a rock and a two-drop). Ship 1-landers and 5+ land hands with nothing to do.
- A keep needs ramp OR a two-drop creature, AND at least one payoff (a lord, Jinnie Fay, Maskwood Nexus, Felidar Retreat, a token maker).
- Auto-keeps: Sol Ring + 2 lands + anything; 3 lands + Cultivate/Signet + King of the Pride/Feline Sovereign.
- Ship: three taplands + only 5-6 drops (Camaraderie, Felidar Sovereign, Collective Blessing); hands of only pump spells and protection with no creatures; a hand whose only green source is a bounce land.

## Wincons

1. **Alpha strike (most games).** 8-12 creatures under two or three lords, then **Overwhelming Stampede** (+X/+X where X is your biggest power, trample), **Titanic Ultimatum** (+5/+5, first strike, trample, lifelink), **Mirror Entity** (X mana: everything becomes X/X) or Jetmir at nine creatures for double strike. **Skyhunter Strike Force** gives everything melee while Rin and Seri is out (+3/+3 attacking all three opponents). Kutzil/Marisi stop instant-speed answers; **Heroic Intervention** covers the rest. **Mirri, Weatherlight Duelist** lets each opponent block with only one creature.
2. **Rin and Seri burn.** {R}{G}{W}, T: damage to any target equal to Dogs you control, life equal to Cats. With Maskwood Nexus that's your whole board to the face once per turn, and it dodges combat. **Rising of the Day** gives it haste the turn it lands.
3. **Felidar Sovereign.** Win at upkeep with 40+ life. Lifelink tokens (Regal Caracal, **Leonin Warleader**, **Cubwarden**, **Pride Sovereign**), True Conviction, **Camaraderie**, **Pledge of Unity** and the commander's activation get there fast; keep protection up for the upkeep.
4. **Infinite tokens (rare, five pieces).** Rin and Seri + Maskwood Nexus + **Whitemane Lion** + **Elven Chorus** + Jinnie Fay: cast Lion ({1}{W}, it is a Cat and a Dog, so two token triggers), Lion returns itself to hand, Jinnie makes the two tokens 2/2 haste Cats, Elven Chorus taps them for the {1}{W} to recast. Mana-neutral, arbitrarily many tokens; tap Rin and Seri to kill one player, the rest swing next turn. Without Chorus it's still a mana sink: 2 mana per token (two with Maskwood), plus 1 life each off Ajani's Welcome.

## Watch out for

- **Board wipes.** Indestructible from **Unbreakable Formation** / **Rootborn Defenses** / Heroic Intervention beats destroy effects; nothing beats Farewell or Cyclonic Rift except sandbagging creatures. Don't dump the whole hand after turn 5 — hold two bodies back.
- **Kutzil doesn't stop abilities**, only spells. Marisi's goad + Mirri's "only one creature can attack you while tapped" clause: with Kaheera/Jetmir vigilance Mirri never taps, so that defensive clause is off; hold her back if you need it.
- **Whitemane Lion saves the commander.** Flash it in response to removal to bounce Rin and Seri; recasting from hand skips commander tax (903.8). Selfless Savior does the same against destroy effects.
- **Cubwarden onto Rin and Seri:** put it *under* so the commander stays a 4/4 with lifelink (rule 730.2a, top component's characteristics); still a Cat spell, so it also makes a Dog.
- **Feline Sovereign + Maskwood** gives your Cats protection from Dogs, and Maskwood makes Cubwarden a Dog spell, so it can no longer mutate onto your other creatures.
- If Rin and Seri dies twice, pivot: the lords and token spells (**Release the Dogs**, **White Sun's Zenith**, **Felidar Retreat**) function without it; at 8 mana the recast is rarely worth it.
- Politics: point Marisi's goaded boards at the player holding the most removal.

## Notable

- **Elven Chorus** is doing double duty: cast creatures from the top plus every token taps for mana, so a wide board turns into 10+ mana for a Mirror Entity activation.
- **Growing Ranks**, **Collective Blessing** and **Temple of the False God** are the weakest slots: slow, expensive or often dead. **Rogue's Passage** is awkward in a go-wide deck.
- **Pack Leader** looks thin with five real Dogs, but with Maskwood it makes the whole team damage-proof on attack.
- Upgrades (all EDHREC staples for this commander, all under $5): **Path to Exile** and **Beast Within** for interaction, **Skullclamp** for card draw off 1/1 tokens, **Impact Tremors** as a non-combat finisher, **Nature's Lore** over a Talisman. Cut Growing Ranks, Collective Blessing, Temple of the False God.
