## Gameplan

Mono-red Goblin tokens. **Krenko, Mob Boss** doubles your Goblin count every time he untaps; the other 99 exist to (a) have a pile of Goblins before he lands, (b) give him haste so he taps the turn he arrives, and (c) turn a wide board into damage without winning combat. About 20 cards make tokens (**Krenko's Command**, **Hordeling Outburst**, **Mogg War Marshal**, **Beetleback Chief**, **Siege-Gang Commander**, **Legion Warboss**, **Goblin Rabblemaster**...), five give Krenko haste (**Goblin Warchief**, **Torch Courier**, **Battle Cry Goblin**, **Reckless Bushwhacker**, **Goblin War Party**), and the damage engines are **Impact Tremors**, **Purphoros, God of the Forge**, **Witty Roastmaster**, **Goblin Bombardment**, **Kyren Negotiations**, **Raid Bombardment** and **Goblin War Strike**.

Typical game: turns 1-3 deploy cheap Goblins and a token sorcery, ideally Warchief plus Tremors or Purphoros. Turn 3-4 Krenko comes down hasty and taps for 4-6 tokens. Turn 5-6 he taps again for 10+, the enter triggers chunk everyone, and War Strike or Bombardment finishes whoever is left. Late, the **Staff of Domination** loop (below) makes the count arbitrarily large.

33 lands, curve peaks at 1-3 (17/14/20 spells). Ramp is thin (**Sol Ring**, **Skirk Prospector**, two land-fetch trinkets); card flow is rummaging (**Faithless Looting**, **Thrill of Possibility**, **Tormenting Voice**, **Electric Revelation**, **Rob the Archives**) plus **Goblin Ringleader**. Removal: **Abrade**, **Chaos Warp**, **Fissure**, **Street Spasm**, **Shattering Pulse**, **Volley Veteran**. No Game Changers; one three-card infinite that rarely lands before turn 7. Bracket 2, plays like a 3 at a precon table.

## Turn 1

Best: **Sol Ring**, then Skirk Prospector, Torch Courier, **Foundry Street Denizen**, **Mogg Fanatic**, **Goblin Javelineer**, Faithless Looting. Dream opener: T1 Mountain + Sol Ring, T2 Mountain + Warchief, T3 Mountain + Krenko (Warchief makes him {1}{R}{R}), tap immediately, Impact Tremors or Krenko's Command next. Without Sol Ring: T1 Prospector or a one-drop, T2 Krenko's Command / **Goblin Instigator** / Mogg War Marshal, T3 Warchief, T4 hasty Krenko tapping for 5+.

## Mulligan

Keep 2-4 lands (3 ideal; 25 Mountains, so color is never the issue). A keep needs a Goblin or token spell castable by turn 2 AND either a haste source or a damage enchantment. Auto-keeps: 3 lands + Warchief, Sol Ring + anything, Prospector + Tremors/Purphoros. Ship: 5+ lands with only top-end (**Warstorm Surge**, Fissure, **Battle Squadron**, **Flamewave Invoker**); all pump/tricks (**You See a Pair of Goblins**, **Goblin War Drums**, War Party) and no bodies; 1-landers even with Looting; 2-landers whose cheap cards are all draw spells rather than Goblins.

## Wincons

1. **Purphoros / Impact Tremors + Krenko taps** (most games). With N Goblins one activation deals 2N (Purphoros) or N (Tremors) to each opponent; Roastmaster adds N. Two activations from 5 Goblins is 10 then 20 from Purphoros alone. Needs Krenko untapped two turns running, or haste on the first.

2. **Wide alpha strike.** Warchief/Battle Cry Goblin haste, War Drums menace, You See a Pair (+2/+0), War Party entwined (+1/+1 haste and 3 tokens), Purphoros's {2}{R} pump, Raid Bombardment for a point per attacking token. War Strike (damage = Goblin count) and Siege-Gang pings close it out.

3. **Infinite: Krenko + Skirk Prospector + Staff of Domination.** Tap Krenko for N, sacrifice four tokens to Prospector for RRRR, pay {3} to Staff to untap Krenko and {1} to untap Staff. Each loop nets N-4 Goblins, so you need 5 Goblins on board (Krenko and Prospector count) to grow; then it's unbounded Goblins and red mana. Convert with Bombardment (1 per sac), Kyren Negotiations (tap each token for 1; summoning sickness is irrelevant, rule 302.6 only restricts a creature's own {T} abilities), Tremors/Purphoros on entry, Siege-Gang ({1}{R}, sac: 2 damage), or Flamewave Invoker with infinite mana. Staff also draws the deck at 6 Goblins a card to find a payoff. **Goblin Matron** and **Goblin Recruiter** find Prospector; nothing finds Staff.

4. **Empty the Warrens** after a cheap-spell turn and **Krenko, Tin Street Kingpin** growing on attack are backup engines.

## Watch out for

- Krenko has zero protection in the list. Expect him to eat the first removal spell: cast him last in a turn, with haste, and tap at once. One activation has already paid for him.
- Wipes are the real killer. Hold Goblin Bombardment so tokens die on your terms; Prospector mana in response only matters with an instant to sink it into (Electric Revelation, Street Spasm, Shattering Pulse buyback). Purphoros is indestructible.
- Forced attacks: Rabblemaster makes your other Goblins, Krenko included, attack. Activate Krenko in the beginning of combat step, after the Rabblemaster/Warboss token trigger, so he's tapped before attackers are declared. **Goblin Assault** forces every Goblin on the table to attack, opponents' too.
- Enchantment removal on Tremors/Purphoros hurts; your only answer to opposing enchantments is Chaos Warp. Artifact hate on Staff/Sol Ring is their only combo answer.
- Recruiter stacks your library with Goblins: only stack what you'll draw in the next two turns or you stop drawing lands. A fetchland or Matron shuffle resets it.
- Commander tax bites with two ramp pieces. After the second Krenko death, pivot to Tin Street Kingpin, Warboss, Rabblemaster and Goblin Assault as engines with Tremors/Purphoros as the clock.
- Politics: Goblin counts are public; 4 Goblins and an untapped Krenko reads as lethal next turn, so you become the archenemy after the first double. Aim War Strike and Kyren at whoever holds the wipe.

## Notable

- Warstorm Surge at 6 mana deals 1 per 1/1 token; Impact Tremors does that for 2. Cut candidate.
- **Goblin Traprunner** (coin flips), Flamewave Invoker (8 mana for 5) and **Seer's Sundial** (no landfall engine) are the weakest cards here.
- **Spikeshot Goblin** is 1 damage a turn unless pumped; **Goblin Burrows** makes it 3. Repeatable answer to mana dorks.
- Street Spasm overloaded is a one-sided wipe of non-fliers, the best card nobody sees coming.
- Maybeboard: **Thornbite Staff** + Krenko + any sac outlet (Bombardment or Prospector) is a two-card infinite from the command zone; **Conspicuous Snoop** + **Kiki-Jiki** needs Recruiter to stack Kiki on top. Either moves the deck to bracket 3.
- Upgrades (EDHREC, cheap): **Goblin Chieftain** ($6, haste anthem, in 77% of lists), **Skullclamp** ($5, converts tokens to cards), **Swiftfoot Boots** or **Lightning Greaves** ($2-4) for Krenko, **Brightstone Ritual** ($2.50) as real ramp. Swap out Warstorm Surge, Traprunner, Sundial and Flamewave Invoker.
