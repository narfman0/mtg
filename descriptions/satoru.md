## Gameplan

**Satoru Umezawa** gives every creature card in hand ninjutsu {2}{U}{B}, and the deck is a delivery system for that text: fourteen cheap evasive bodies (**Slither Blade**, **Triton Shorestalker**, **Mist-Cloaked Herald**, **Changeling Outcast**, **Tormented Soul**, **Gudul Lurker**, **Ornithopter**, **Faerie Seer**, **Spectral Sailor**, **Gingerbrute**, **Baleful Strix**, plus **Tetsuko Umezawa, Fugitive**, **Thassa, God of the Sea** and **Creeping Tar Pit** to unblock others) swing in, then get swapped for an 8-to-12-drop you would never hardcast. Satoru's trigger digs three deep once a turn, so every ninjutsu also fixes your hand. **Silver-Fur Master** makes all of it cost 1 less.

Payoffs are enters/combat-damage cards, never "whenever attacks" cards: a creature put onto the battlefield attacking never "attacked" (rules 508.3a/508.4), so **Grave Titan** makes its Zombies from its enters trigger only. The cheats: **Blightsteel Colossus** (11 infect, one hit kills), **Jin-Gitaxias, Core Augur** (draw seven each end step, opponents discard to zero), **Consecrated Sphinx**, **Ancient Silver Dragon**, **Razaketh, the Foulblooded** (sac dorks to tutor), **Toxrill, the Corrosive**, **Valgavoth, Terror Eater**, and the real ninjas (**Fallen Shinobi**, **Silent-Blade Oni**, **Ink-Eyes, Servant of Oni**, **Nashi, Moon Sage's Scion**, **Higure, the Still Wind**, **Yuriko, the Tiger's Shadow**).

Typical game: turns 1-2 dork and rock, turn 3 Satoru, turn 4 swing and ninjutsu a bomb. Turns 5-7 you draw 3-10 cards a turn off **Rhystic Study**, **Mystic Remora**, **Ingenious Infiltrator**, **Reconnaissance Mission**, **Grazilaxx, Illithid Scholar** and Sphinx while holding **Counterspell**, **Negate**, **Flusterstorm**, **Drown in the Loch**, then close with a Blightsteel hit, a Yuriko drain, or an unanswerable card gap.

Bracket 4: six Game Changers (**Cyclonic Rift**, **Rhystic Study**, **Demonic Tutor**, **Vampiric Tutor**, **Imperial Seal**, **Consecrated Sphinx**) plus **Sol Ring**, **Dark Ritual**, **Lotus Petal**, but no infinite combo. 34 lands, 8 rocks/rituals, 6 removal, 4 counters, 2 wipes (**Toxic Deluge**, Rift).

## Turn 1

Best: land + Sol Ring + a 1-drop, or land + any unblockable 1-drop. Next: **Preordain**, **Brainstorm**, **Mystic Remora** (it's best on turn 1-2), Lotus Petal into a dork. Imperial Seal/Vampiric turn 1 should fetch a draw engine or protection, not Blightsteel with no board.

Ideal opener: T1 Island, Slither Blade. T2 land + **Dimir Signet**/**Arcane Signet**/**Talisman of Dominance** and Tetsuko or Silver-Fur, or T2 Satoru off Sol Ring or Swamp + Island + Dark Ritual. Once Satoru lands, never cast the big creatures.

## Mulligan

Keep 3-4 lands (2 only with Sol Ring, Petal or a Signet). A keep needs an evasive 1-2 drop plus either a draw piece (Rhystic, Remora, Strix, Preordain) or one bomb to ninjutsu; Satoru is always available, so lands + dorks + one bomb is fine. Ship: 5+ lands and only fat; hands whose creatures are all 6+ MV (nothing to swing with); 1-landers even with Dark Ritual; hands whose only early plays are removal. Blightsteel in the opener justifies keeping only if the hand can attack on turn 3.

## Wincons

1. **Blightsteel Colossus** via ninjutsu (4 mana, 3 with Silver-Fur). It enters attacking and unblocked (blockers are already declared, 509.1h); 11 infect = 10+ poison = that player loses (704.5c). Indestructible, and an activated ability can't be Counterspelled, so only bounce, exile or a fog stops it. Multiply it: **Sakashima's Student** ninjutsu ({1}{U}) enters as a copy of Blightsteel; **Thousand-Faced Shadow** ninjutsu makes a token copy of the attacking Blightsteel, tapped and attacking. Each copy's controller picks which player it attacks (508.4), so three unblocked dorks and 10 mana (7 with Silver-Fur) kill all three opponents in one combat. Find it with Demonic Tutor, Vampiric, Imperial Seal, Razaketh or Satoru's dig; it shuffles back if discarded, so Reanimate can't get it.
2. **Yuriko** drain. A Ninja connecting reveals your top card and everyone loses its MV. Vampiric/Imperial Seal/Brainstorm putting Blightsteel (12), Jin (10) or Valgavoth (9) on top before damage drains each opponent for 9-12 and hands you the bomb. Satoru's dig resolves on activation, before damage, so stack the top after it.
3. Card-advantage lock: Jin-Gitaxias on turn 4-5, Sphinx, Ancient Silver Dragon, Rhystic + Remora, then counter everything and finish with unblockable beats, Grave Titan, Toxrill shrinking every blocker, or **Mist-Syndicate Naga** doubling per hit.
4. Theft/value grind: Silent-Blade Oni casts their best card free, Fallen Shinobi plays their top two, Ink-Eyes and **Archpriest of Shadows** reanimate on damage, Higure tutors a Ninja per hit.
5. **Kaito Shizuki**'s emblem takes five turns; treat him as a draw engine.

## Watch out for

- Satoru is the engine; without him the top end rots in hand. Protect with **Lightning Greaves**/**Swiftfoot Boots**, hold Counterspell for removal on him, and fall back on the ninjas with printed ninjutsu (Infiltrator, **Moon-Circuit Hacker**, Yuriko, Student, Higure, Ink-Eyes). After two removals, hardcast the 5-7 drops and draw engines instead.
- Timing: ninjutsu only after blockers are declared, and it still works after combat damage (the dork stays "unblocked" until end of combat). Let Hacker/Infiltrator/Strix connect and draw, then swap them for Grave Titan or Jin: no damage from the ninja, but you get the enters trigger, Satoru's dig, and the dork back to recast for another Strix draw or Seer scry.
- Only the first ninjutsu each turn triggers Satoru. Cards with printed ninjutsu also have Satoru's; pay the cheaper. Once activated, ninjutsu resolves even if Satoru dies in response (113.7a).
- Wipes and pingers wreck the dorks; keep two in hand. Toxic Deluge kills your own board, so X=1 for tokens or only when far behind.
- Real problems: Propaganda-style taxes, fogs, Settle-style exile, flash reach blockers versus Ornithopter/Seer, and open blue mana when you commit to Blightsteel (bounce is the answer). Torpor Orb stops Titan/Strix/Shadow but not combat damage.
- Politics: you look durdly until turn 4. Don't Vampiric with an untapped board of dorks in the open; the table reads it.

## Notable

- **Thassa** rarely animates (devotion 5), but {1}{U} makes Grave Titan or Toxrill a ninjutsu enabler themselves.
- **Cut Down** and **Drown in the Loch** are weak versus bombs; Loch is dead early (6% play rate). **Feed the Swarm** is the only enchantment answer, so save it for taxes and Rhystic mirrors.
- Gingerbrute needs {1} per swing; Naga is often just a 3-mana body.
- Upgrades (cheap, EDHREC): **Invisible Stalker** (hexproof unblockable), **Siren Stormtamer** (1-drop flier that counters removal on Satoru), **Rune-Scarred Demon** (ninjutsu = 4-mana tutor). From the maybeboard, **Archon of Cruelty** and **Hullbreaker Horror** are ideal ninjutsu targets and **Fierce Guardianship**/**Force of Will** raise the interaction count. Cuts: Kaito, Drown in the Loch, Naga.
