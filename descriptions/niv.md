## Gameplan

Izzet spellslinger-control built around one engine: **Niv-Mizzet, Parun**. Every instant or sorcery anyone casts draws you a card; every card you draw pings for 1. The 99 exists to land Niv on turn 4-5 behind counters, keep him alive, and turn "draw a card" into a kill. 34 lands, 7 rocks (**Sol Ring**, both Signets, **Fellwar Stone**, **Talisman of Creativity**, **Thought Vessel**, **Simian Spirit Guide**), ~20 one-mana cantrips/interaction, 12 counters, 9 spot-removal spells, 2 wipes (**Blasphemous Act**, overloaded **Cyclonic Rift**).

Early: cantrip, rock, hold a counter; stick **Mystic Remora** or **Rhystic Study** if the table allows. Mid: resolve Niv (he can't be countered — only removal matters), then chain cantrips; each **Opt** becomes "draw 2, deal 2." Late: find an aura or the second dragon and end it in one turn, or wheel with Niv out.

Bracket 4. Eight Game Changers (**Consecrated Sphinx**, **Cyclonic Rift**, **Fierce Guardianship**, **Gamble**, **Mystical Tutor**, **Rhystic Study**, **The One Ring**, **Underworld Breach**) plus two-card infinites with the commander, tutorable by **Merchant Scroll**, **Muddle the Mixture**, **Drift of Phantasms** and Gamble.

## Turn 1

**Sol Ring** (Niv on 4), **Mystic Remora** (draws 3-5 in a cheap-spell pod before you stop paying), or a cantrip: **Ponder**, **Preordain**, **Brainstorm**, **Consider**, **Opt**, **Gitaxian Probe**. Hold **Mental Misstep** for their Sol Ring or tutor.

Ideal: land, Sol Ring, Remora; turn 2 land, **Izzet Signet**/**Arcane Signet**, cantrip with **Counterspell** or **Swan Song** up. That's Niv on turn 3 with a full grip, or turn 4 with a counter held.

## Mulligan

Keep 3-4 lands (2 only with Sol Ring or a Signet). Niv costs UUURRR: count duals, fetches and Signets toward three of each colour, not just land count. A keep needs lands plus at least one of ramp, cantrip, or cheap counter. Sol Ring, Remora, **Mystical Tutor** or Merchant Scroll plus a counter is a snap keep.

Ship: five lands and two 6-drops; all-red hands (nothing to do before Niv); **Curiosity** + **Laboratory Maniac** with no cantrips or rocks (dead until turn 5); one land + Sol Ring + Signet + cantrips unless a cantrip costs one mana — it must hit land 2 on the draw.

## Wincons

1. **Niv + Curiosity / Ophidian Eye / Tandem Lookout.** Niv pings an opponent, the aura or soulbond draws, Niv pings again. Curiosity and Eye are "may draw" so you stop before decking; Lookout's draw is mandatory, so stop by pinging a non-opponent. Deals roughly your library size in damage — aim it at one or two players. With **Laboratory Maniac** out, the last draw from an empty library wins outright. Eye has flash: cast it end of turn or in response to removal.

2. **Niv + Niv-Mizzet, Visionary.** Parun pings, Visionary draws (mandatory), Parun pings. Same stop rule. Two 6-drops, so usually the turn after Niv lands.

3. **Underworld Breach + Frantic Search + three lands making {2}{U}.** Escape Frantic Search for {2}{U} exiling three; it draws 2, discards 2, untaps three lands, returns to the yard. Graveyard stays level, so it loops through your whole library. With Niv that's 3 damage per loop; **Guttersnipe**, **Fiery Inscription**, **Electrostatic Field** and **Thermo-Alchemist** hit every opponent per cast; **Storm-Kiln Artist** makes a Treasure per cast; Lab Maniac just wins. Works without Niv.

4. **Wheels with Niv.** **Windfall**, **Wheel of Misfortune**, **Reforge the Soul**: draw 7 = 7 pings plus the cast trigger. **Harmonic Prodigy** doubles both Niv triggers (he's a Wizard); **Veyran, Voice of Duality** doubles the cast-trigger draw; Prodigy + Curiosity goes exponential. **The Locust God** turns each draw into a hasty 1/1 flyer.

5. Fair: Niv 5/5 flying plus Guttersnipe/Fiery Inscription/Thermo-Alchemist chip while counters hold the table.

## Watch out for

Niv is the whole deck. Cheap creature removal, **Pyroblast**/Red Elemental Blast (destroys him for one mana), edicts and exile are the problems; tax at 8 and 10 is where games are lost. Saves: **Snap** and **Chain of Vapor** bounce him in response; **Pongify**/**Rapid Hybridization** on your own Niv beats exile; **Otawara** channels for 3 with a legend out; **Deflecting Swat** is a free redirect. Once he's down, counters go on removal, not their threats.

Graveyard hate blanks Breach and **Treasure Cruise**. Rule-of-Law and Drannith Magistrate effects turn off the spellslinger half. Lifegain stalls the Curiosity loop. Silence effects stop the combo turn — keep **Negate**/**Swan Song** for them.

Sequencing: cantrip after Niv is in play, not before, unless digging for lands. Blasphemous Act kills Niv (13 to each creature): bounce him first or accept the recast. Wheel of Misfortune with Niv out: never pick the lowest number or you don't wheel; the highest bidder eats that number. Reforge the Soul miracles for {1}{R} on your first draw of a turn — a Rhystic or Niv trigger on an opponent's turn wheels at instant speed. **Mystic Sanctuary** needs three other Islands; play basics first.

Politics: Niv draws off everyone's spells, so the table focuses him fast. Remora and Rhystic paint the target early — hold them until you can protect them.

## Notable

**Consecrated Sphinx** + any wheel is 14+ cards. **Flame of Anor** is two modes here: six Wizards (both Nivs, **Archmage Emeritus**, Prodigy, Lab Maniac, Veyran), so it draws 2 and kills something. **Mana Drain** into Niv is the classic turn-3 line.

Underperformers: **Simian Spirit Guide** is one-shot mana in a deck that isn't storming off on turn 2. **Fabled Passage** enters tapped when it matters. **Electrostatic Field** is the weakest pinger.

Upgrades (EDHREC): **Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind** (46%, +37% synergy, cheap) is a fourth Curiosity partner and combos with Visionary — cut Spirit Guide. **Goblin Electromancer** makes the one-mana cantrips free and every Frantic Search loop mana-positive. **Chaos Warp** or **Abrade** answers noncreature permanents, which the deck lacks outside Flame of Anor and Pyroblast. **Flusterstorm** is in the mail: cut Gitaxian Probe or Electrostatic Field for it.
