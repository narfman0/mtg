## Gameplan

**Norin the Wary** exiles himself whenever anyone casts a spell or attacks and returns at the next end step: a free creature ETB on every turn of every round. The deck taxes that ETB with pingers that hit each opponent (**Impact Tremors**, **Purphoros, God of the Forge**, **Witty Roastmaster**, **Agate Instigator**, **Molten Gatekeeper**, **General Kreat, the Boltbringer**, **Weftstalker Ardent**, **Slash, Reptile Rampager**) or a target (**Raphael, Tough Turtle**, **Shocking Sharpshooter**, **Terror of the Peaks**, **Warstorm Surge**). **Genesis Chamber** makes every Norin return a second body, **Panharmonicon** doubles every trigger, and **Ojer Axonil, Deepest Might** (red noncombat pings become 4), **Torbran, Thane of Red Fell**, **Hawkeye, Young Avenger** and **Mechanized Warfare** multiply it.

Cheap token spells (**Dragon Fodder**, **Krenko's Command**, **Hordeling Outburst**, **Mogg War Marshal**, **Siege-Gang Commander**, **Tempt with Vengeance**, **Goblin Rabblemaster**, **Urabrask's Forge**) feed the same pingers in bursts; **Skullclamp**, **Goblin Bombardment** and **Outpost Siege** convert bodies into cards or face damage. Card flow is red rummaging plus **Tome of Legends**, which pages up on every Norin return.

Typical game: T1 Norin, T2-3 a pinger and Chamber/Tremors, T4-5 an amplifier or Purphoros while dumping tokens. By T6-7 each Norin return is 8-16 damage across the table and one token spell or a **Grinning Ignus** sink ends it. 34 lands, average nonland cost 2.9, ~15 draw effects, ~9 ramp, 9 removal (4 sweepers: **Blasphemous Act**, **Chain Reaction**, **Fiery Confluence**, **Fury**). No Game Changers, tutors, infinites or extra turns: Bracket 2 by the rules, kills like a solid Bracket 3.

## Turn 1

Mountain, **Norin the Wary** — every turn he is not out is a lost ETB. With **Sol Ring** or **Simian Spirit Guide**, add a 2-drop: Tremors, Agate, Raphael, Sharpshooter, Chamber, Skullclamp or **Springleaf Drum** (Drum can tap summoning-sick Norin; rule 302.6 only restricts the creature's own tap abilities).

Ideal start: T1 Norin. T2 Chamber or Tremors (both off Sol Ring) — Norin plus his Myr now ping at every end step. T3 Roastmaster / Gatekeeper / Kreat plus a two-token spell: 6+ per opponent per round with nothing on the stack.

## Mulligan

- Keep 3-4 lands (2 lands + Sol Ring/**Arcane Signet**/**Mind Stone** counts). Ship 2-landers without a rock and 5+ landers without a 2-drop.
- A keep needs a pinger (Tremors, Agate, Raphael, Sharpshooter, Roastmaster, Gatekeeper, Kreat, Ardent, Purphoros, Chamber) or a rummage spell plus a token spell. Norin is guaranteed, so the trigger source is always there.
- Auto-keep: lands + Sol Ring + pinger; lands + Chamber + Tremors; lands + Skullclamp + Mogg War Marshal.
- Ship: only amplifiers (Torbran, Ojer, Hawkeye, Mechanized) with no pinger — they multiply zero; three rummage spells and two lands; hands whose only action costs 5.

## Wincons

1. **Attrition pings (most games).** Norin (+ Myr) re-enters every turn and each pinger hits every opponent; with Ojer each 1-damage ping is 4. Two pingers + Ojer + Chamber = 16 to each opponent per round; Panharmonicon doubles it.
2. **Token burst.** Hordeling Outburst into Tremors + Purphoros + Roastmaster + Torbran is (3+4+3) x 3 = 30 to each opponent for three mana. Tempt with Vengeance gives X ETBs (more if opponents accept). **Impulsive Pilferer** encore is 3 ETBs + 3 Treasures; Siege-Gang is 4 ETBs.
3. **Grinning Ignus sink.** {R}, bounce: add {C}{C}{R}; recast for {2}{R}. Net {R} per loop, each loop an ETB (two with Chamber). **Rose Room Treasurer** refunds the first two loops each turn with Treasures, then lets you pay X to burn a face — with Ojer, X=1 is 4. Ojer + Tremors + Purphoros = 8 to each opponent per {R}. Not infinite, but 5-6 spare mana closes a game. Sorcery speed.
4. **Goblin Bombardment.** Sacrifice the board at instant speed: each token is 1 (3 with Torbran, 4 with Ojer) to any face. Outpost Siege on Dragons adds a ping per creature leaving, Norin included. Always sacrifice in response to a wipe.
5. **Fiery Confluence** triple face mode: 6, or 12 with Torbran or Ojer, to each opponent. **Beatdown** (Rabblemaster, Slash, Terror, Purphoros pumps, **Ogre Battledriver** haste) is a real backup; attacking exiles Norin anyway.

## Watch out for

- **Norin dodges spell-based removal.** Casting the spell triggers his exile; the trigger goes on the stack above the spell and resolves first (603.3), so wraths, Swords and even split-second spells (702.61b) miss him. What kills him: activated/triggered abilities (planeswalker minuses, Ballista, ability-based edicts), counterspells, and -X/-X statics like Elesh Norn that kill him on every return.
- **Never move Norin to the command zone from exile.** Rule 903.9a is a "may": decline it. Moved, he is a new object (400.7) and the return trigger loses him.
- **Opponents order replacement effects (616.1).** A 1-damage ping with Ojer + Torbran is 4, not 6 (they apply Torbran first, then the floor). Ojer alone = 4; Torbran + Hawkeye + Mechanized = 6. Ojer for pinger swarms; the additive trio for big hits (Terror, Surge, Rose Room X, Confluence).
- **Torpor Orb / Hushbringer effects** turn the deck off. Answers: **Abrade**, **Vandalblast**, **Shattering Spree**, Confluence for artifacts; Fury, Abrade, Chain Reaction for creatures. Hold **Pyroblast** for a counter on Purphoros/Ojer or for Cyclonic Rift. Only **Chaos Warp** touches enchantments, so lifegain enchantments and Aura of Silence are real problems.
- **Your own sweepers** exile Norin on cast, so he survives them. Chamber gives opponents Myr too: bigger Chain Reaction, but free chump blockers against Rabblemaster.
- **Sequencing.** Cast tokens and creatures precombat; Norin's end-step return is a bonus regardless. Miracle **Reforge the Soul** needs the first draw of the turn (Outpost Siege Khans exiles, so it does not interfere). Skullclamp on Norin: he dies, you draw two, recast for R plus tax.
- **Politics.** Symmetric pingers make you the archenemy by T5; aim the targeted ones at whoever is closest to winning.
- **Commander removed repeatedly.** Recasting is cheap, and tokens plus Rabblemaster/**Goblin Assault**/Urabrask's Forge keep the pingers fed without him. **Tokka & Rahzar, Unsupervised** turns his every exile into a Treasure and a counter.

## Notable

- **Aether Revolt**: Norin leaving turns revolt on every turn, so it is +2 noncombat damage for four mana; the energy clause never fires. Lowest play rate in the list (8%) and Torbran does it better — first cut.
- Goblin Assault and Rabblemaster force 1/1s to attack into blockers, which fights holding bodies for Bombardment — second cut.
- **Idol of Oblivion** draws nearly every turn here; Springleaf Drum and Simian Spirit Guide exist to land Norin plus a pinger a turn early.
- Upgrades (EDHREC): **Ruby Medallion** (74% of Norin decks, ~$14) makes every Ignus loop net a colorless mana; **Gimli of the Glittering Caves** (65%, ~$15) grows off every Norin return; **Solphim, Mayhem Dominus** (54%, ~$25) doubles noncombat damage, same ordering caveat as Ojer. Cheap: **Confusion in the Ranks** (65%) if the table likes chaos, **Lightning Bolt** as amplified removal.
