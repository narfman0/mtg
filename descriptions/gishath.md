## Gameplan

Naya dinosaur stompy with a real enrage sub-engine. The plan is simple: ramp hard (35 lands, 11 land-ramp spells, three rocks), land a discounted **Gishath, Sun's Avatar** on turn 3-4, and convert 7 trample-haste damage into free dinosaurs off the top. Five cost reducers all hit the commander: **Urza's Incubator** and **Kinjalli's Caller** together make Gishath cost 5; **Herald's Horn**, **Otepec Huntmaster** and **Marauding Raptor** stack on top of that.

The second layer is self-damage. **Wrathful Raptors** turns every point of damage dealt to your dinosaurs into face damage, **Wayta, Trainer Prodigy** doubles every enrage/damage trigger and fights two of your own creatures for {G}, and **Zacama, Primal Calamity**, **Marauding Raptor**, **Chandra's Ignition** and **Blasphemous Act** are the damage sources. Enrage payoffs are **Ripjaw Raptor** (draw), **Ranging Raptors** (land), **Trapjaw Tyrant** (exile) and **Apex Altisaur** (fight chain).

Typical game: turns 1-3 rocks, ramp and reducers; turn 3-4 Gishath swings into a still-empty board; turns 5-7 the free dinos plus **Garruk's Uprising**, **Elemental Bond**, **The Great Henge** and **Vaultborn Tyrant** bury the table in cards, and a finisher below closes. Bracket 3, upper end: three Game Changers (**Ancient Tomb**, **Teferi's Protection**, **Worldly Tutor**), no infinite combo, no mass land denial (**Star of Extinction** only hits one land).

## Turn 1

Best openers: **Sol Ring**, **Ancient Tomb** (into **Arcane Signet**/**Fellwar Stone** on turn 1), or **Kinjalli's Caller**. Dream sequence: T1 Tomb + Sol Ring + Signet, T2 land + Urza's Incubator, T3 land + Gishath for 6 (Tomb 2 + Sol 2 + 2 lands) and swing for 7 with haste. Normal good hands: T1 land + Sol Ring, T2 **Nature's Lore**/**Three Visits**/**Farseek** plus **Otepec Huntmaster**, T3 Incubator or **Cultivate**, T4 Gishath.

## Mulligan

Keep 3-4 lands, or 2 lands with Sol Ring/Tomb plus a 2-mana ramp spell. A keep needs both a way to reach 6-8 mana by turn 4 (ramp or a reducer) and something to do before Gishath (**Regal Imperiosaur**, **Topiary Stomper**, **Wayward Swordtooth**, **Hulking Raptor**, **Rhythm of the Wild**). Snap-keep any hand with Incubator + Caller/Otepec plus lands. Ship: five lands and two 7-drops, or 2 lands + three dinosaurs and no ramp spell. Traps: a fistful of protection (**Heroic Intervention**, **Flawless Maneuver**, Teferi's Protection) with no ramp, and **Marauding Raptor** as your only reducer next to Otepec (Raptor's 2 damage kills Otepec on entry).

## Wincons

1. **Fair trample beatdown.** Gishath hits, reveals 7, drops 2-3 dinos; **Regisaur Alpha** and Rhythm of the Wild give them haste, **Quartzwood Crasher** and **Ghalta and Mavren** (12/12; makes an X/X trampler every attack) snowball. **Goring Ceratops** gives the team double strike, so Gishath connects in both damage steps (CR 510.4) and triggers twice; **Akroma's Will** with Gishath out grants double strike *and* protection from each color, making the team effectively unblockable. **Rogue's Passage** forces Gishath through.
2. **Wrathful Raptors damage math.** With Raptors out, Wayta's {G} fight between two of your fatties (Ghalta and Mavren vs Zacama: Ghalta takes 9, Zacama takes 12 and dies, each trigger doubled) is 42 face damage split however you like. Chandra's Ignition on Ghalta and Mavren deals 12 to each opponent, then every other dino dealt 12 sends 12 (24 with Wayta) at a face. Blasphemous Act/Star of Extinction with Raptors and N dinos on board is 13N/20N damage to non-dinosaur targets. Note 603.10: all these triggers are checked immediately after the damage event, so Raptors and Wayta count even if the wipe kills them.
3. **Poison.** **Etali, Primal Conqueror** transformed (10 mana, Zacama untap or **Itlimoc** covers it) is a 7/7 indestructible trampler giving poison; Goring Ceratops double strike is 14 poison in one swing.
4. **Apex Altisaur chain.** Ten power fights on entry and again on every enrage; with Wayta each fight is two, eating an entire board.

## Watch out for

Gishath is the counterspell magnet: cast it off **Cavern of Souls** naming Dinosaur, and Rhythm of the Wild makes every creature uncounterable. Wraths hurt most; keep Heroic Intervention/Flawless Maneuver/Teferi's Protection up once you have 3+ dinos (Flawless Maneuver and **Deflecting Swat** are free with a commander out). Edicts, exile and theft beat the protection suite; a stolen Ghalta and Mavren ends you. Stax outgrinds you, and you are usually the fastest deck at the table, so expect to be targeted.

Sequencing: Zacama's untap only works "if you cast it" -- cheating it in via Gishath, **Savage Order**, **Finale of Devastation**, **Defense of the Heart** or **Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant** gives no untap. **Pantlaza** discovers only once a turn, so pick the highest toughness dino entering (Ghalta 12 = free anything). Marauding Raptor kills Otepec, **Ghalta and Mavren**'s 1/1 Vampire tokens and **Bonehoard Dracosaur**'s 3/1 tokens, but it also fires Trapjaw (free exile), Ripjaw (draw) and Ranging Raptors (land) on entry -- Wayta doubles each. Zacama's {2}{R} on your own Trapjaw/Ripjaw is repeatable exile/draw; one ping per turn on Trapjaw is safe (5 toughness), two need **Regal Imperiosaur**. **Congregation at Dawn** or Worldly Tutor at end of turn stacks dinos for Gishath's reveal; Etali, Primal Storm's attack trigger also eats the top card, so order them. **Mosswort Bridge** unlocks with Gishath plus almost any dino.

When Gishath eats removal repeatedly, stop recasting at 10+ and pivot: Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant dumps the hand, Finale X=8 finds it, and the Wrathful Raptors lines kill without the commander.

## Notable

Off-meta but pulling weight: Wayta (not on EDHREC's Gishath list) is the best card in the deck once Raptors or Trapjaw is out; **Tranquil Frillback** and Zacama are your only artifact/enchantment answers, so hold Frillback for a real target. **Defense of the Heart** (6%) fires every game against creature decks. Underperformers: **Battlefield Forge** (painland in the wrong colors), **Fellwar Stone**, and **Curious Altisaur** (a 2/5 in a deck already full of draw). Star of Extinction kills your own board unless Raptors is out.

Upgrades: **Polyraptor** ($16) with Marauding Raptor is a mandatory infinite token loop (a drawn game unless Wrathful Raptors is out, which makes it infinite damage) -- add only if the table is fine with a true combo; **Secluded Courtyard** or **Unclaimed Territory** for Battlefield Forge; **Ghalta, Primal Hunger** ($1, usually costs {G}{G}) for Fellwar Stone; **Temple Altisaur** if you find your own wipes/pings killing the team too often.
