## Gameplan

Simic lands: ramp into **Tatyova, Benthic Druid** on turn 4, then play two to four lands a turn and draw a card off each. Extra land drops come from **Azusa, Lost but Seeking**, **Dryad of the Ilysian Grove**, **Exploration**, **Burgeoning**, **Oracle of Mul Daya**, **Icetill Explorer**, **Aesi, Tyrant of Gyre Strait**, **Loot, Exuberant Explorer**, **Wayward Swordtooth** and **Druid Class**. **Sakura-Tribe Scout** and **Llanowar Scout** drop lands without using a land play (305.4). Lands never run out: **Ramunap Excavator**, **Conduit of Worlds**, **Ancient Greenwarden** and **Icetill Explorer** play them from the graveyard, and **Life from the Loam** and **Splendid Reclamation** bring them back in bulk. Eight fetch-style lands (**Misty Rainforest**, **Prismatic Vista**, **Myriad Landscape**, **Brokers Hideout** and friends) give two landfalls each.

Landfall turns into a board. **Avenger of Zendikar** grows every Plant, **Rampaging Baloths**, **Zendikar's Roil** and **Scute Swarm** make bodies, **Mossborn Hydra** doubles, **Field of the Dead** makes Zombies once you have seven land names (the deck has 24), and **Lotus Cobra** / **Tireless Provisioner** make mana. Greenwarden doubles all of it (603.2d).

Typical game: turns 1-3 are ramp and a land-drop engine, turn 4 is Tatyova, turns 5-6 bring a landfall threat while Tatyova refills your hand. Turn 6-7 is the kill: **Scapeshift** or **Craterhoof Behemoth** onto a board that survived a turn. 38 lands, about 30 ramp or extra-land pieces, average nonland mana value 3.0. Three Game Changers (**Cyclonic Rift**, **Crop Rotation**, Field of the Dead), no two-card combos: high Bracket 3. Two three-card loops (see Wincons) lean toward low 4, so mention them before the game.

## Turn 1

Best openers: **Exploration** or **Burgeoning**; **Sol Ring**; a Scout. Otherwise **Explore** or **Growth Spiral** turn 2, then **Rampant Growth**, **Farseek**, **Nature's Lore** or **Three Visits**. Those Forest searches can grab **Breeding Pool**, **Hedge Maze**, **Sodden Verdure** or **Tangled Islet** as well as basics. Ideal start: T1 Exploration, T2 Lotus Cobra or Azusa with two lands down, T3 Tatyova. Realistic start: T2 two-mana ramp, T3 **Cultivate** / **Kodama's Reach** / Dryad, T4 Tatyova with a land to play right after.

## Mulligan

Keep 3-5 lands with a ramp spell or an extra-land-drop engine. Tatyova is the card engine, so the opener only has to reach turn 4 with lands to spare; a land-heavy hand is fine here. Auto-keep: Exploration or Burgeoning plus lands; Sol Ring plus a three-drop ramp. Ship: two lands with no one- or two-mana ramp; hands with all payoffs (Avenger, Craterhoof, Baloths) and no acceleration. Don't keep a hand just because it has one loop piece; the deck has no way to tutor for the rest.

## Wincons

1. **Big landfall turn onto an existing board.** Avenger's Plants that survived a turn gain +1/+1 per land; **Scapeshift** for eight lands (fetch Field and fetches for more triggers) is eight counters on every Plant, eight Tatyova cards and eight Baloths, all at once. Mossborn Hydra with a trample path is 2^n counters after n landfalls.
2. **Craterhoof Behemoth.** +X/+X and trample to every creature. Only Craterhoof itself has haste, so it's the finish for tokens, Plants and Zombies that were already on the board last turn, not for ones made this turn.
3. **Loop A: Retreat to Coralhelm + a Scout + a bounce land** (**Simic Growth Chamber**, **Guildless Commons**, or **Oboro, Palace in the Clouds** paying {1}). The Scout puts the land in, the land returns itself, and Coralhelm untaps the Scout. The Scout must have been yours since the turn began (302.6). That's unbounded landfall: with Avenger on board from last turn it's an arbitrarily large Plant army; with Provisioner, unbounded Treasure.
4. **Loop B: Kodama of the East Tree + a landfall token maker + a bounce land.** Kodama puts the bounce land in (which doesn't retrigger Kodama, 603.4), landfall makes a token, and the token triggers Kodama. Order the bounce land's trigger to return itself first. Same results as Loop A.
5. **Field of the Dead** as a slow grind: every land after seven names is a 2/2.

**Tatyova's draw isn't optional.** Stop any loop with cards left in the library, or you lose (704.5b).

## Watch out for

- **Graveyard hate** (Rest in Peace, Bojuka Bog) turns off Ramunap, Conduit, Greenwarden, Icetill, Loam and Reclamation. It slows the deck but doesn't stop it.
- **Search hate** (Aven Mindcensor, Opposition Agent) punishes the fetches and roughly a dozen ramp searches. Kill it with **Pongify** / **Rapid Hybridization** before cracking lands.
- **Wipes.** Hold **Heroic Intervention** once the board is worth protecting. Druid Class level 3 makes a land a creature, which a wipe takes out.
- **Decking.** Greenwarden doubles Tatyova's draws. Big Scapeshifts and loops with Greenwarden out can draw 20+ cards; count your library first.
- **Greenwarden plus a bounce land** doubles the bounce trigger, so two lands go back to hand.
- **Burgeoning** triggers on an opponent *playing* a land, not on lands put onto the battlefield.
- **Crop Rotation** is an instant: fetch in response to land destruction, or at an opponent's end step for Field or Oboro.
- **Wayward Swordtooth** can't attack or block until you have ten permanents (ascend).
- **Mystic Sanctuary** enters untapped with Dryad out (every land is an Island) or with **Spelunking**, which rebuys a Rift or a Scapeshift.

## Notable

The land base now has four Forest Island duals (Breeding Pool, Hedge Maze, Sodden Verdure, Tangled Islet), so Three Visits, Nature's Lore and **Skyshroud Claim** find real fixing. Keep the 16 basics: about 15 cards search for them. **Boseiju, Who Endures** is removal in a land slot. Cards that look cuttable but earn their slot: Life from the Loam, Guildless Commons and Wayward Swordtooth all have low EDHREC inclusion but feed the engine. Upgrades: **Walking Ballista** or **Finale of Devastation** turn the loops into deliberate kills, so treat either as a step into Bracket 4. **Fierce Guardianship** over **Negate** is the fourth Game Changer. **Traveling Chocobo** (20% of Tatyova decks) doubles landfall. **Scaled Herbalist** is a third Scout, which also means a third Loop A piece.
